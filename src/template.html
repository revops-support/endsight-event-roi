How each number is worked out
Events listed
HubSpot campaigns with the Event date property filled in. The credit window runs from the Event date for 30, 60, 90 or 180 days.
Contact Type
The contact's own Type property: Client counts as Customer, Partner or Reseller as Partner, Prospect as Prospect, any other value as Other. A blank Type shows as Unknown.
Influenced contact
Anyone HubSpot counts as influenced by the campaign, plus anyone with a touch traced below. Deleted or merged contacts drop out.
Attended
Scanned into the event's Blinq campaign. The scan is dated by when the contact joined the Blinq list, or the Event date if that is not known.
Engaged
Opened or clicked a marketing email on the campaign, or submitted a form on the campaign, between the campaign start and the end of the window. Meetings are an outcome the event is credited with, not a sign of engagement, so a meeting never moves a contact up a tier or makes an open deal influenced.
Targeted
Only added to a list on the campaign, or counted by HubSpot through a touch this page cannot trace yet, such as a sequence enrollment, page view or ad interaction.
Meetings
Any meeting created from the Event date through the end of the window with at least one influenced contact. Meetings typed as internal or personal are left out. Held: start time has passed and the outcome is not canceled or no-show. Upcoming: start time still ahead. The Type and influence shown are the strongest among the meeting's contacts.
Deals created
Created from the Event date through the end of the window with at least one influenced contact, at any influence level.
Deals influenced
Already open on the Event date (created before it and not closed before it), with a contact who attended or engaged between the campaign start and the end of the window, while the deal was open. Targeted-only touches do not count here.
Shared credit
When more than one event credits the same deal or meeting in its window, each gets an even share. Weighted credit by touch type will replace this once the weights are agreed.
Return on ad spend
Credited won MRR from deals created, divided by the campaign's spend total in HubSpot.
Associated contacts by stage
HubSpot's own influenced contacts for the campaign over the last 365 days, by current lifecycle stage, matching the HubSpot report of that name.
Email phase
Send date before the Event date is pre-event; on or after is post-event. Results are lifetime totals for each email.
Dates
All dates are in the HubSpot account's time zone.
